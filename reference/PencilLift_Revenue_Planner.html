Interpret the result

With 1,000 families, 40 billable parent impressions each at an assumed $3 eCPM yield $120. If 20% of those families each generate $25 of qualifying book sales at 4.5%, theoretical affiliate income adds $225. Combined: $345/month, or $0.345 per active family before extra costs.

The same family might never see an ad or buy anything. Use the average across all active families when planning your subscription subsidy. Pending contracts, estimated commissions and actual cash must remain separate in the app's reporting.

If a fixed sponsor buys this inventory, count the sponsor fee instead of network impressions for that slot. Selling, reviewing, serving and reconciling campaigns also has a cost.

Learning rewards stay independent. Never award points, discounts or extra scans for affiliate clicks or purchases.
